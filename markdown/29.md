# 29 — Goal Programming for Recruitment (Jieli Company)

## 1. Original Problem

Jieli Company recruits for **2 branches** (Donghai, Nanjiang) and **3 specialties**.
There are **6 applicant types**; each type has an available headcount, a list of specialties it can handle, a preferred specialty, and a preferred branch.

Priorities (lexicographic):
- $p_1$: cover the staffing demand for every branch × specialty.
- $p_2$: 8000 people end up in their preferred specialty (target $Q_2 = 8000$).
- $p_3$: 8000 people end up in their preferred branch (target $Q_3 = 8000$).

Question: at least how many people fail to satisfy $p_3$?

| Table 4-3 (demand $d_{r,s}$) | Spec 1 | Spec 2 | Spec 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Donghai | 1000 | 2000 | 1500 |
| Nanjiang | 2000 | 1000 | 1000 |

| Type $t$ | $n_t$ | $\mathcal{S}^{\text{cap}}_t$ | $s^{\text{pref}}_t$ | $r^{\text{pref}}_t$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1500 | {1,2} | 1 | Donghai |
| 2 | 1500 | {2,3} | 2 | Donghai |
| 3 | 1500 | {1,3} | 1 | Nanjiang |
| 4 | 1500 | {1,3} | 3 | Nanjiang |
| 5 | 1500 | {2,3} | 3 | Donghai |
| 6 | 1500 | {3}   | 3 | Nanjiang |

## 2. Sets / Indices

- $\mathcal{R}$ — branches, $|\mathcal{R}| = R = 2$.
- $\mathcal{S}$ — specialties, $|\mathcal{S}| = S = 3$.
- $\mathcal{T}$ — applicant types, $|\mathcal{T}| = T = 6$.
- $\mathcal{S}^{\text{cap}}_t \subseteq \mathcal{S}$ — specialties that type $t$ can handle.

## 3. Parameters

| Symbol | Shape | Description |
|---|---|---|
| $d_{r,s}$ | $R \times S$ | Demand for specialty $s$ at branch $r$ |
| $n_t$ | $T$ | Number of applicants of type $t$ |
| $s^{\text{pref}}_t$ | $T$ | Preferred specialty of type $t$ |
| $r^{\text{pref}}_t$ | $T$ | Preferred branch of type $t$ |
| $Q_2$ | scalar | Target for $p_2$ ($= 8000$) |
| $Q_3$ | scalar | Target for $p_3$ ($= 8000$) |

## 4. Decision Variables

| Symbol | Domain | Shape | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| $x_{t,r,s}$ | $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ | $T \times R \times S$ | Number of type-$t$ applicants assigned to specialty $s$ at branch $r$ |
| $u_{r,s}$ | $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ | $R \times S$ | Unmet demand |
| $v_2$ | $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ | scalar | Shortfall against target $Q_2$ |
| $v_3$ | $\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ | scalar | Shortfall against target $Q_3$ |

## 5. Objective — Preemptive (Lexicographic)

Minimize in priority order $p_1 \succ p_2 \succ p_3$:

$$
\text{lex-min} \;\; \Bigg(\; \underbrace{\sum_{r}\sum_{s} u_{r,s}}_{p_1},\;\; \underbrace{v_2}_{p_2},\;\; \underbrace{v_3}_{p_3}\;\Bigg).
$$

(That is, solve sequentially: first minimize $\sum u_{r,s}$; fix this optimum, then minimize $v_2$; finally fix both and minimize $v_3$.)

## 6. Constraints

**(C1) Assign Only to Allowed Specialties:**

$$
x_{t,r,s} \;=\; 0 \qquad \forall t,\, \forall r,\, \forall s \notin \mathcal{S}^{\text{cap}}_t.
$$

**(C2) Do Not Exceed Available Headcount:**

$$
\sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} x_{t,r,s} \;\le\; n_t \qquad \forall t \in \mathcal{T}.
$$

**(C3) Demand Balance — No Overstaffing, Track Shortfall** ($u_{r,s}$ is the unmet portion):

$$
\sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} x_{t,r,s} \;+\; u_{r,s} \;=\; d_{r,s} \qquad \forall r \in \mathcal{R},\, \forall s \in \mathcal{S}.
$$

**(C4) Definition of Preferred-Specialty Shortfall:**

$$
\sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}} x_{t,r,\, s^{\text{pref}}_t} \;+\; v_2 \;=\; Q_2.
$$

(Here $\sum_t \sum_r x_{t,r, s^{\text{pref}}_t}$ counts applicants actually placed in their preferred specialty.)

**(C5) Definition of Preferred-Branch Shortfall:**

$$
\sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} x_{t,\, r^{\text{pref}}_t,\, s} \;+\; v_3 \;=\; Q_3.
$$

**(C6) Domains:**

$$
x_{t,r,s} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0},\quad u_{r,s} \ge 0,\quad v_2 \ge 0,\quad v_3 \ge 0.
$$

## 7. Auxiliary Reporting (Not Decision Variables)

- Number of applicants placed in their preferred specialty: $A_2 = \sum_t \sum_r x_{t,r, s^{\text{pref}}_t} = Q_2 - v_2$.
- Number of applicants placed in their preferred branch: $A_3 = \sum_t \sum_s x_{t,\, r^{\text{pref}}_t,\, s} = Q_3 - v_3$.
