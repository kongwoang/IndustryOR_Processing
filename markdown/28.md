# 28 — Multi-period Investment with Lock-up and Investment Caps

## 1. Original Problem

A person has 300,000 yuan and is offered four investment opportunities over a 3-year horizon:

1. **Project 1** — invest at the beginning of every year (years 1, 2, 3); 20% profit; principal + interest released after 1 year and reinvestable.
2. **Project 2** — invest at the beginning of year 1 only; redeemable at the end of year 2 with a 150% gross return; cap of 150,000.
3. **Project 3** — invest at the beginning of year 2; redeemable at the end of year 3 with a 160% gross return; cap of 200,000.
4. **Project 4** — invest at the beginning of year 3; redeemable after 1 year with a 140% gross return; cap of 100,000.

Goal: maximize **principal + interest at the end of year 3**.

## 2. Sets / Indices

- $\mathcal{T} = \{1, 2, \dots, T\}$ with $T = 3$ — set of periods.
- $\mathcal{J} = \{1, 2, \dots, N\}$ with $N = 4$ — set of investment opportunities.
- $\mathcal{A}_j \subseteq \mathcal{T}$ — opening periods of project $j$.

## 3. Parameters

| Symbol | Description | Value (example) |
|---|---|---|
| $C_0$ | Initial capital | 300,000 |
| $d_j$ | Lock-up duration (in periods) | $d = (1, 2, 2, 1)$ |
| $\rho_j$ | Gross return multiplier at maturity | $(1.2, 1.5, 1.6, 1.4)$ |
| $U_j$ | Cumulative investment cap ($+\infty$ if uncapped) | $(\infty, 150{,}000, 200{,}000, 100{,}000)$ |
| $\mathcal{A}_j$ | Set of allowed opening periods | $\{1,2,3\},\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}$ |

## 4. Decision Variables

| Symbol | Domain | Shape | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| $x_{j,t}$ | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ | $N \times T$ | Amount invested in project $j$ at the beginning of period $t$ |
| $h_t$ | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ | $T+1$ (indices $t=1,\dots,T+1$) | Liquid capital at the start of period $t$ (after maturities of previous periods are released, before new investments of period $t$) |
| $Z$ | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ | scalar | Total capital at the end of period $T$ |

## 5. Objective Function

$$
\max \; Z \;=\; h_{T+1}.
$$

## 6. Constraints

**(C1) Initial Capital:**

$$
h_1 \;=\; C_0.
$$

**(C2) Capital Balance Across Periods** — for $t = 1, 2, \dots, T$:

$$
h_{t+1} \;=\; h_{t} \;-\; \sum_{j=1}^{N} x_{j,t} \;+\; \sum_{j=1}^{N} \sum_{\substack{\tau \in \mathcal{T}\\ \tau + d_j = t+1}} \rho_j\, x_{j,\tau}.
$$

(Meaning: liquid capital at the start of the next period = liquid capital at the start of this period − new investments made this period + all matured principal and interest released at the start of the next period.)

**(C3) Per-period Liquidity Limit:**

$$
\sum_{j=1}^{N} x_{j,t} \;\le\; h_t \qquad \forall t \in \mathcal{T}.
$$

**(C4) Investment Window:**

$$
x_{j,t} \;=\; 0 \qquad \forall j \in \mathcal{J},\; \forall t \notin \mathcal{A}_j.
$$

**(C5) Cumulative Investment Cap per Project:**

$$
\sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} x_{j,t} \;\le\; U_j \qquad \forall j \in \mathcal{J}.
$$

(If $U_j = \infty$ the constraint is dropped.)

**(C6) Non-negativity:**

$$
x_{j,t} \ge 0,\qquad h_t \ge 0,\qquad Z \ge 0.
$$

## 7. Worked Instance ($T = 3$, problem data)

Expanding (C2) explicitly:

$$
\begin{aligned}
h_2 &= h_1 - (x_{1,1} + x_{2,1}) + \rho_1 x_{1,1} \\
h_3 &= h_2 - (x_{1,2} + x_{3,2}) + \rho_1 x_{1,2} + \rho_2 x_{2,1} \\
h_4 &= h_3 - (x_{1,3} + x_{4,3}) + \rho_1 x_{1,3} + \rho_3 x_{3,2} + \rho_4 x_{4,3}
\end{aligned}
$$

with $\rho = (1.2, 1.5, 1.6, 1.4)$ and $h_1 = 300{,}000$. Objective: $\max h_4$.
