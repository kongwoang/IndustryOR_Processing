# Index 22 — Production Planning with Activation Costs and Minimum Batches

## 1. Original Problem (questions_txt/22.txt)

A company produces 3 types of products, $A_1, A_2, A_3$, over $T = 22$ working days per month. For each product $p$, we have:

- $D_p$: maximum demand (in units of 100 kg).
- $S_p$: selling price per unit.
- $C_p$: production cost per unit.
- $Q_p$: maximum daily production quantity if the line is dedicated to this product.
- $F_p$: fixed cost to activate the production line for one day.
- $B_p$: minimum batch size when activated.

Requirement: create a plan that specifies which product to produce on which day and in what quantity to **maximize net profit**.

| Product | $A_1$ | $A_2$ | $A_3$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $D_p$ | 5300 | 4500 | 5400 |
| $S_p$ | 124 | 109 | 115 |
| $C_p$ | 73.30 | 52.90 | 65.40 |
| $Q_p$ | 500 | 450 | 550 |
| $F_p$ | 170,000 | 150,000 | 100,000 |
| $B_p$ | 20 | 20 | 16 |

## 2. Sets / Indices

- $\mathcal{P} = \{0, 1, \dots, P-1\}$ — set of products, $P = 3$.
- $\mathcal{T} = \{0, 1, \dots, T-1\}$ — set of production days, $T = 22$.

## 3. Parameters

| Symbol | Shape | Description |
|---|---|---|
| $D_p$ | $P$ | Demand ceiling for product $p$ |
| $S_p$ | $P$ | Unit selling price for product $p$ |
| $C_p$ | $P$ | Unit production cost for product $p$ |
| $Q_p$ | $P$ | Maximum daily production quantity |
| $F_p$ | $P$ | Daily activation cost |
| $B_p$ | $P$ | Minimum batch size when activated |

## 4. Decision Variables

| Symbol | Domain | Shape | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| $x_{p,t}$ | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ | $P \times T$ | Production quantity of product $p$ on day $t$ |
| $y_{p,t}$ | $\{0,1\}$ | $P \times T$ | Equals 1 if product $p$ is activated on day $t$ |

## 5. Objective Function

$$
\max \; z \;=\; \sum_{p=0}^{P-1}\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} \Big[ (S_p - C_p)\, x_{p,t} - F_p\, y_{p,t} \Big]
$$

## 6. Constraints

**(C1) Production-Activation Link (includes "no production if not activated" and "do not exceed quota when activated"):**

$$
x_{p,t} \;\le\; Q_p\, y_{p,t} \qquad \forall p \in \mathcal{P},\; \forall t \in \mathcal{T}.
$$

**(C2) Minimum Batch Size When Activated:**

$$
x_{p,t} \;\ge\; B_p\, y_{p,t} \qquad \forall p \in \mathcal{P},\; \forall t \in \mathcal{T}.
$$

(When $y_{p,t}=0$, the constraint becomes $x_{p,t} \ge 0$ — which is non-binding; when $y_{p,t}=1$, it forces $x_{p,t} \ge B_p$.)

**(C3) Total Production Does Not Exceed Demand:**

$$
\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} x_{p,t} \;\le\; D_p \qquad \forall p \in \mathcal{P}.
$$

**(C4) Variable Domains:**

$$
x_{p,t} \ge 0,\qquad y_{p,t} \in \{0,1\} \qquad \forall p \in \mathcal{P},\; \forall t \in \mathcal{T}.
$$
